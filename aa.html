Account & Data Deletion Request

This page allows users of the ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha application to request deletion of their account and eligible personal information.

How account deletion works

To protect your account from unauthorized deletion, ISKCON may verify that the request belongs to the registered user before processing it.

1
Enter your details Provide your name and the mobile number registered with your ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha account.
2
Send the deletion request Tap the button below. Your email application will open with a prepared account deletion request.
3
Verification ISKCON may contact you or request reasonable verification to confirm that you are the account holder.
4
Deletion After verification, eligible account information will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

This webpage itself does not automatically delete your account. It allows you to initiate a deletion request directly with ISKCON.

What information may be deleted?

After verification and subject to applicable requirements, eligible information associated with your account may include:

  • Account/profile information
  • Name and contact information associated only with the account
  • Profile photograph
  • Optional profile information
  • Eligible application account records
  • Other personal information that is no longer required

Information that may need to be retained

Certain information may need to be retained even after account deletion where required or permitted for legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, audit, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

  • Donation records
  • Transaction and payment reference records
  • Donation receipts
  • Applicable 80G / Form 10BE records
  • PAN-related records where legally required
  • Accounting and audit records
  • Fraud-prevention and security records where necessary
Retained information will only be kept for legitimate legal, accounting, regulatory, security, or related purposes and should not be used to continue an active application account.
