SpendGrid : Budget & Wealth OS is engineered as a zero-cloud, 100% offline personal finance utility. We do not own remote servers, we do not require account logins, and your sensitive financial transactions never leave your smartphone hardware.

🎯 1. Core Application Identity & Purpose

SpendGrid is an on-device personal financial management (PFM) application designed to help users track expenses, manage cashflow, organize personal budgets, and identify financial trends without exposing their data to external cloud services.

We operate strictly on a "No-Account, No-Telemetry, Zero-Tracking" architecture. We believe personal financial privacy is a fundamental human right.

📱 2. Requested Device Permissions & Exact Technical Reasons

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Financial Data Guidelines, SpendGrid requests only the minimum permissions required for automated on-device bookkeeping:

A. SMS Access (READ_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS) Financial Management
  • Core Functional Purpose: To read incoming verified bank debit and credit text messages (e.g., HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Jio Payments, Slice, Sadhana Bank) to automatically log your transactions without manual typing.
  • Scope of Data Read: Only messages containing verified institutional bank headers, masked account digits (e.g. x1185, XX8619), and financial transaction amounts are parsed.
  • Strictly Excluded: Personal chats, family messages, social media OTPs, passwords, and marketing spam are 100% ignored and discarded instantly.
  • On-Device Processing: All text parsing occurs client-side using local regular expressions (Regex). Zero SMS contents are ever transmitted over the network.
B. Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) Real-Time Auto-Sync
  • Core Functional Purpose: When financial institutions or UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, JioFinance, CRED, Chase, Revolut, Monzo) do not send an SMS for micro-payments (e.g., ₹10 chai or $2 transit), this listener captures the live push alert in 0.01 seconds.
  • Scope: Only payment confirmation notifications containing monetary amounts and transaction verbs are processed. Messaging and social apps are strictly ignored.
  • Zero Background Transmission: Captured notification titles and bodies are stored solely in your local device storage.
C. Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO) [Optional] Voice Command Logger
  • Purpose: Used exclusively when you tap the floating Mic button to speak a quick expense (e.g. "Twenty rupees auto cash").
  • Processing: Converted to text in real-time using Android's native on-device SpeechRecognizer. No audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded.
D. Scoped Media Storage (Android MediaStore API) Export Backup & PDF
  • Purpose: Allows you to export your data as a .CSV backup or generate an official .PDF statement saved directly in your device's Download/SpendGrid/ folder.
E. Haptic Feedback (VIBRATE) Tactile UI
  • Purpose: Triggers subtle 25ms tactile haptic clicks when buttons are pressed for an authentic native experience.

🛡️ 3. What We Access vs What We NEVER Access

🚫 What SpendGrid NEVER Touches:
  • We NEVER read your personal WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS conversations.
  • We NEVER capture or store banking passwords, UPI PINs, CVV codes, or full card numbers.
  • We NEVER upload your contacts, photos, or location data.
✅ What SpendGrid Reads Strictly:
  • Financial transaction debit & credit amounts.
  • Merchant / Beneficiary names (e.g. Swiggy, Uber, Amazon, JioFinance).
  • 12-Digit NPCI UTR / Reference numbers to prevent duplicate entries.

📢 4. Third-Party Advertising & Ad Networks

To keep SpendGrid free for all users, we may display third-party advertisements served by verified ad networks (such as Google AdMob).

  • Standard Identifiers: Ad networks may collect standard device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) and coarse diagnostics to serve relevant ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Zero Financial Data Sharing: Advertising partners have ZERO ACCESS to your bank SMS, UPI logs, account balances, or financial records. Your financial ledger is strictly sandboxed on your device hardware.
STRICT ZERO DATA-SELLING PLEDGE

We do not sell, rent, monetize, trade, or transfer your personal or financial data to telemarketers, loan companies, data brokers, credit bureaus, or advertising networks. Your data remains strictly isolated inside Android's private app sandbox.

🗑️ 5. User Sovereignty & Complete Data Deletion

You retain 100% control over all information logged inside the application:

  • Individual Deletion: You can delete any single transaction or multiple selected transactions anytime with 1-click.
  • Flag & Block Senders: You can permanently block specific unwanted sender IDs directly from the transaction inspector.
  • Permanent Factory Reset: Tapping "Clear All Data & Reset" in Settings permanently and irreversibly erases all records from local storage.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application completely removes all local database files from your device.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SpendGrid : Budget & Wealth OS is intended strictly for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect, parse, or process personal or financial data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

❓ 7. Frequently Asked Questions (Security FAQs)

🔒 Can SpendGrid read my banking OTPs?
No. SpendGrid's on-device regex engine matches only financial debit/credit records and automatically discards one-time passwords (OTPs) and personal messages.
📱 Does SpendGrid upload my bank transactions to any server?
No. SpendGrid does not own or connect to any cloud server. All records are saved exclusively in your phone's internal storage.
💾 How do I backup my data when changing phones?
Go to Settings ➔ Export Backup (.CSV). The file will be saved directly into your Download/SpendGrid/ folder, which you can easily transfer to your new phone.
